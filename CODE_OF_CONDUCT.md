# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Use clear, professional, and evidence-based language.
- Critique ideas, methods, and results rather than people.
- Credit prior work and contributors appropriately.
- Treat numerical and scientific uncertainty transparently.
- Respect confidentiality for unpublished research material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, plagiarism, deliberate misrepresentation of results, and sharing confidential material without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer or the corresponding author of the associated manuscript. Reports will be handled discretely and in good faith.
