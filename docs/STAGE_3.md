# Stage 3 — Make edge physics measurable

## Objective

Stage 3 turns the qualitative finite-boundary evidence in the prototype notebook into reproducible,
quantitative ribbon diagnostics.  The stage establishes the finite-system part of the article's
bulk-to-boundary bridge:

$$
\text{bulk band Chern numbers}
\longrightarrow
\text{gap Chern numbers}
\longrightarrow
\text{edge-localized ribbon branches}
\longrightarrow
\text{oriented reference-energy crossings}.
$$

The implementation is deliberately restricted to static boundary physics.  Real-space packet
preparation, time evolution, currents, leakage, and group-velocity validation remain later stages.

## Implemented numerical objects

### Edge strips and participation

For an x-open ribbon, Stage 3 defines disjoint strips of width $w$ at the left and right edges.
For a normalized eigenstate $\psi_n$,

$$
P_{\mathrm{left}}^{(n)}=\sum_{m<w}|\psi_n(m)|^2,
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{right}}^{(n)}=\sum_{m\ge L_x-w}|\psi_n(m)|^2,
$$

and

$$
P_{\mathrm{edge}}^{(n)}=P_{\mathrm{left}}^{(n)}+P_{\mathrm{right}}^{(n)}.
$$

The module also records the edge polarization
$P_{\mathrm{left}}-P_{\mathrm{right}}$ and the inverse participation ratio (IPR).  The IPR is
reported as a secondary localization diagnostic; it is not used alone to label an edge state.

### Ribbon spectrum

The central static geometry is periodic along $y$ and open along $x$.  The same canonical
Stage-1 Harper-Hofstadter parent Hamiltonian is used through `qhe.models.ribbon_hamiltonian`; no
separate sign, phase, or hopping convention is introduced in Stage 3.

`qhe.boundary.diagonalize_ribbon` returns a structured `RibbonSpectrum` containing the full
$E_n(k_y)$ spectrum, eigenvectors, side-resolved edge participation, polarization, and IPR on a
closed-open $k_y$ grid.

### Gap Chern numbers and oriented crossings

Given Stage-2 FHS band Chern numbers, the gap Chern number below the $j$-th gap is

$$
C_{\mathrm{gap},j}=\sum_{n\le j} C_n.
$$

Global bulk gaps are measured from the periodic Bloch bands.  A reference energy is chosen at the
midpoint of every positive global gap.  The code then searches the finite ribbon for crossings of
that reference energy.  A crossing is retained only when its linearly interpolated
$P_{\mathrm{edge}}$ exceeds the configured threshold.  Its orientation is the sign of
$dE/dk_y$, and its side is inferred from the larger interpolated left/right participation.

For the frozen $\phi=1/3$ convention, the Stage-3 baseline verifies

$$
(C_1,C_2,C_3)=(-1,2,-1),
\qquad
(C_{\mathrm{gap},1},C_{\mathrm{gap},2})=(-1,1).
$$

With the present ribbon/Fourier convention, the signed left-edge crossing counts equal
$(-1,1)$, while the right-edge counts equal $(+1,-1)$.  The opposite signs on opposite
boundaries are expected for a finite strip.

## Acceptance criteria

Stage 3 is complete only when all of the following hold for the reference $\phi=1/3$ workflow.

- The periodic bulk gaps used for the crossing search are strictly positive.
- Ribbon eigenstates have measured, not visually guessed, left/right/total edge participation.
- Every accepted reference-energy crossing exceeds the edge-participation threshold.
- Each global gap contains one identified localized crossing on each boundary.
- The side-resolved oriented crossing counts agree with the cumulative Stage-2 gap Chern numbers
  under the frozen convention.
- The result is regenerated by a command-line acceptance script and regression-tested.

## Scope limitation

This is a finite-ribbon numerical diagnosis of bulk-boundary correspondence.  It does not replace
a theorem-level derivation, does not claim disorder robustness, and does not yet establish chiral
wave-packet motion.  Those are intentionally deferred until the dynamics stage.
